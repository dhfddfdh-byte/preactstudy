# Pre-ACT Practice

A study site for the Pre-ACT, Form 608 (fall 2026). It shows one question at a time, with the passage beside it, and scores you on the Pre-ACT's 1–35 scale.

## What it does

- **Sessions:** a full test, or English, Math, Reading, or Science on its own. Each runs in two modes:
  - **Timed:** the official time limits.
  - **Practice:** no time limit, and you check each answer as you go.
- **Redo missed:** a drill of only the questions you got wrong last time in a section.
- **Test tools:**
  - a timer you can hide
  - pause
  - flag for review
  - answer eliminator
  - highlighter
  - line reader
  - text size
  - Desmos graphing calculator on Math
- **Saving:** progress saves automatically in your browser. Close the tab and you can resume where you left off.
- **Scores:** section scores, Composite (average of English, Math, Reading) and STEM (average of Math and Science). Your scores are shown against the readiness benchmarks and a goal you set, and each section earns a star when it reaches its benchmark.
- **Light and dark mode**, and a layout that works on phones.

## The booklet

The test booklet isn't in this repo. Each person opens their own copy of the Form 608 booklet PDF in the site. The file is kept in that browser only and never uploaded.

`geometry.js` holds only the page coordinates of each question, answer choice, and passage, so the site can cut the booklet into one question at a time. It's generated by `tools/geometry.py`.

## Scoring notes

- **Answer key:** taken from the official September 2026 score report.
- **Tryout questions:** 30 questions are unscored tryout items. They're shown and checked, but left out of scale scores, like on test day:
  - English 31–42
  - Math 5, 9, 22, 30
  - Reading 17–24
  - Science 13–18

  ACT didn't release their answers, so those were worked out from the booklet.
- **Scale conversion:** ACT doesn't publish this form's raw-to-scale table. The conversion is estimated and matches the official report exactly at those September scores.
